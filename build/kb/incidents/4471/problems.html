Not one issue. A fix for the parser closes the parser problem only. The other two stay open until they have tickets and fixes of their own.

parser: dates read month first

The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a booking for 7 April comes out as 4 July. These reports come in through the app, by email and through booking-v2 forms, at every tenant.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. At a day-first tenant, people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way. These are only reports that came through the form at a booking-v3 tenant. Fixing the parser does not fix them: the hint and the way the form reads its own field have to agree.

one-day move: off by one day, not flipped

The desk set these reports apart in its own notes (desk-notes.json): the date moves by one day, or lands on a different day for different people, instead of swapping day and month. Examples: R-41202 (Tuesday 12 May shown as Monday 11 May), R-41344 (one meeting, a different day for a client in Lisbon and one in Toronto), R-41236 (the invite arrived before the booking was made). The cause is not known yet. Until it is, these reports are a problem of their own, not a footnote under parser.
